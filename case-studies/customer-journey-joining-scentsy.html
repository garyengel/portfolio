Scentsy · Consultant Onboarding

Joining Scentsy

Using journey maps to build an accurate picture of how customers actually decide to join Scentsy as a Consultant, replacing internal assumption with real interviews and survey data.

UX Design Lead Role
Journey Mapping Focus
Interviews · Focus Groups · Surveys Method
Scentsy Company
01 · Journey Map & Interviews

Starting with what we believed, then testing it.

Starting with initial thoughts and internal beliefs, I created a rough journey map as a starting point. Incorporating interviews and focus groups, I was able to add, delete, and change touchpoints and thought processes for customers who wished to join Scentsy as Consultants. Digging further, I uncovered opportunities from the largest pain points, and the company was able to adjust the enrollment program to ease those pain points directly.

02 · The Quantitative Journey

Surveys turned a rough map into a real one.

Further exploring through surveys, I mapped out the journey of a typical customer looking to join Scentsy. Although the resulting map was complex, it helped clarify and challenge the internal belief of what the "typical journey" of a customer looking to join Scentsy actually looks like.
