Scentsy · Subscription & Loyalty

Scentsy Club

Scentsy Club is Scentsy's subscription program that lets customers schedule recurring shipments of "the stuff that runs out" (wax, oils, cleaning, and more) on their own cadence, with subscriber-only perks that reward repeat buying and reduce the risk of lapsing.

Scentsy Company
01 · Planned, Not Occasional

Turning occasional purchases into planned ones.

Scentsy Club transformed the way customers buy and experience Scentsy by shifting purchases from occasional replenishment to planned replenishment, structurally better for retention and revenue stability than one-off orders. It improved the business by creating more predictable repeat demand, strengthening retention through unique subscriber benefits, and scaling from an early, limited VIP concept into a large, durable loyalty engine.

02 · Subscription Management & Rewards

A points system that rewards the loop.

The program added loyalty mechanics beyond simple discounts, via a rewards and referrals points system. Customers earn points on their own subscriptions, and more points on referred subscribers, creating a built-in loop for repeat purchasing and customer-driven acquisition.

A points ledger gave subscribers visibility into what they'd earned and what it was worth, turning an abstract loyalty mechanic into something a Consultant or customer could point to and understand at a glance.
