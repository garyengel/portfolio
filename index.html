About

Two decades of turning research into results.

I've spent my career at the intersection of research and design. I believe you can't design well without first understanding deeply, which is why usability testing and qualitative research sit at the center of everything I do, not at the end.

As UX Design Lead at Scentsy, I manage a team of designers and researchers responsible for end-to-end digital experiences across e-commerce, mobile, and internal consultant tools. I've helped move the organization from instinct-based to evidence-based design decision making.

Case Studies

Projects that moved the needle.

01
Scentsy Home App
Connected Devices Mobile UX Research

Designed Scentsy's first smart-device control app, a ground-up product shipped alongside a new connected fragrance device. Pre-launch research ran under NDA with a hand-selected participant panel to protect the unannounced hardware, which shaped pairing, onboarding, and controls before a single unit was in a store. Post-launch, I led the platform's expansion from single-purpose controller to full ecosystem, adding over-the-air firmware updates, in-app fragrance reordering, device scheduling, and per-device scent and light settings.

7 NDA research participants 3.6 App Store rating OTA update platform

My Process

Research first. Always.

01

Discover & Define

I start with the problem, not the solution. User interviews, stakeholder alignment, competitive landscape, and hard success metrics, before any wireframes exist.

→
02

Explore & Prototype

Divergent thinking across the full solution space, rapid low-fidelity prototypes, and early feedback loops with actual users before committing to direction.

→
03

Test & Iterate

Moderated usability testing with 5–8 participants per round, 2–3 rounds per project. Evidence-based iteration, not gut checks. Every decision is traceable to a finding.

→
04

Deliver & Measure

Precise handoff documentation, close dev collaboration, and post-launch measurement tied to metrics set in discovery. The work isn't done when it ships. It's done when it performs.
